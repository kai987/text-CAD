# R18 attic window and lighting / 阁楼北窗与照明 / 小屋裏北窓と照明

## R18 阁楼居中北窗与照明

阁楼北窗移至屋脊中心；概念北侧屋脊支柱改为窗两侧支柱、上方过梁和上部支柱，避开窗洞。阁楼南北各设一盏短吊灯，发光面位于默认6900 mm剖切线以下，统一由“室内灯光”控制。截面、承载和照明均为演示假设，当前输出以R18为准。

## R18 小屋裏の中央北窓と照明

北窓を棟中心へ移動し、北側の概念棟柱を窓両側の柱・まぐさ・上部柱へ変更して開口を確保。南北各1灯の短い吊灯を標準6900 mm断面より下に配置し、「室内照明」で一括操作。断面・耐力・照明はデモ仮定。現行出力はR18です。

## R18 centred attic north window and lighting

The north attic window moves to the ridge centre. Paired jamb posts, a header and an upper ridge post keep its opening clear in the concept frame. Two short pendants sit below the default 6900 mm attic cut plane and share the Indoor lights switch. Sections, capacity and lighting remain illustrative; current exports are R18.

The retained 600×300 mm top-hung window has X centre 4095 mm and sill FL+850 mm. Attic light diffusers are at Z=6813–6818 mm; spotlight origins Z=6808 mm. Source positions Y=1300 and 5980 mm. The 90 mm jamb/header concept uses 10 mm side gaps and a header 60 mm above the aperture; load transfer, materials and connections are uncalculated.

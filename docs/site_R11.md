## English

Two side-by-side parking bays each measure 2800 × 5000 mm. All shrubs are removed, and the east, west and north margins shrink from 2000 to 1000 mm. The lot measures 10190 × 13780 mm with a geometric area of 140.4182 m². The south margin remains 5500 mm; two garden lights move outside the parking bays. All dimensions are demonstration assumptions. Statutory setbacks, vehicle turning, road access and structural adequacy remain unverified.
